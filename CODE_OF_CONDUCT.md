# Code of Conduct

Adar follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
(version 2.1). In short:

- Be respectful, welcoming and patient — everyone was new once.
- Critique ideas and code, never people.
- No harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, trolling or sharing others' private information.
- Assume good intent and help keep discussions constructive.

These rules apply to issues, pull requests, discussions and any other project space.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer (contact details on
[github.com/mikelgarciaurbina](https://github.com/mikelgarciaurbina)). Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and temporarily or permanently ban participants who
break these rules.
